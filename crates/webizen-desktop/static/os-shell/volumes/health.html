⚕ Health & Care Local records

A private care surface for clinical records, device measurements, and personal exports. Nothing is diagnosed or sent to a vendor by this page.

Device & platform importsLocal only

This is vendor-neutral health intake. The first available CSV adapter recognises Samsung Health exports (steps, sleep, heart rate, and weight); each successful batch is retained as a local Q42 file. Other adapters can be added without changing the person’s record model.

A companion must implement the authenticated Webizen health-bundle protocol. This does not claim direct pairing with a watch.

Apple Health, Health Connect / Google Fit, Fitbit, Garmin, and Google Maps Timeline files are kept as original local attachments for review. Their import adapters are not installed yet.
