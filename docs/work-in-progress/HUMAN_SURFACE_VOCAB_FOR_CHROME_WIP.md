## 1. Four planes (jury-safe)

| Chrome word | Fabric | One-liner | Never collapse into |
|-------------|--------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Who** | NaturalAgent | Living person / principal | DID, wallet, OS account, badge, DNS/ABN, “identity” bag |
| **Claim** | Claim / opinion / attestation | Something asserted — may be signed, may be wrong | Who; VC integrity ≠ truth |
| **Handle** | Spatiotemporal / route / locator | How to reach or place *now* | Who forever; domain death ≠ person death |
| **Tool** | Instrument | Proves, stores, names, or routes | Who; possession ≠ person |

**Cheat card:** Speak **who · claim · handle · tool**. If copy merges two planes, refuse the merge.

### Typing cuts (say once)
- **AI-agent** ≠ person ≠ machine  
- **Machine** = device + network instruments, not who  
- **Organization / persona ficta** ≠ NaturalAgent  
- **Flora / fauna** = living-typed, not personhood by default; not Thing-wash  
- **SHACL-first** living/sacred · **OWL ok** artifacts (Volume, InvokeId, CRS, catalogs)
