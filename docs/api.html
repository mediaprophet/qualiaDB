foundation::plane_feature_guard ADR 0004

Enforces cross-plane non-entailment on symbolic and classifier feature bags (PlaneTag::{Who, Claim, SpatiotemporalHandle, Instrument, Policy}) and the closed 7-variant KeyRoleTag vocabulary. Prevents Who or Instrument identifiers from entering Policy or Claim scoring vectors without explicit governed projection.

q42::execution_profile ADR 0003 · Q42-08

Separates ExactnessProfile (Exact, DeterministicApproximate, InteractiveApproximate) from SolverVerificationOutcome (WitnessChecked, UnsatisfiableWithinDomain, UnknownWithinBudget, GlobalImpossibilityProved). admit_for_policy_decision gates normative and policy admission strictly on verified exact outcomes.
