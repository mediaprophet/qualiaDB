Temporal Reconstruction & Live Motion Producers

test_render_with_auto_temporal_accumulates_across_frames: PASS

The engine provides continuous temporal history accumulation without failing closed. The renderer allocates internal GPU attachments for motion vectors (Rg16Float) and reactive masks (R8Unorm), calculating camera projection deltas and surface reactivity across consecutive frames.

Screen-Space Motion Vectors

Reconstructs world-space position from scene depth and projects into previous camera view-projection coordinates:

let p_ndc = vec4<f32>(uv * 2.0 - 1.0, depth, 1.0); let p_world = inv_view_proj * p_ndc; let p_prev = prev_view_proj * (p_world / p_world.w); let motion = ((p_ndc.xy - p_prev.xy / p_prev.w) * 0.5);

Reactive History Rejection

Dynamically suppresses history ghosting across transparent water surfaces, alpha-cutout vegetation, and high-velocity particles:

let alpha_reactive = select(0.0, 1.0, is_alpha_tested); let water_reactive = select(0.0, 0.8, is_water_surface); let reactive_mask = max(alpha_reactive, water_reactive);
