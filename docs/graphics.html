TEMPORAL RECONSTRUCTION · SCREEN-SPACE VELOCITY & REACTIVE MASKS

Temporal Reconstruction & Live Motion Producers

Multi-Frame History · Rg16Float Velocity + R8Unorm Reactive Mask

The engine provides continuous temporal anti-aliasing and history accumulation across frames without heap allocations in the render loop. Dedicated GPU attachments store screen-space motion vectors (Rg16Float) and surface reactivity masks (R8Unorm), computing camera reprojection deltas and clamping disoccluded neighbourhood history to eliminate ghosting on fast-moving or translucent geometry.

Screen-Space Motion Vectors

Reconstructs world-space position from scene depth and projects into previous camera view-projection coordinates:

let p_ndc = vec4<f32>(uv * 2.0 - 1.0, depth, 1.0); let p_world = inv_view_proj * p_ndc; let p_prev = prev_view_proj * (p_world / p_world.w); let motion = ((p_ndc.xy - p_prev.xy / p_prev.w) * 0.5);

Reactive History Rejection

Dynamically suppresses history ghosting across transparent water surfaces, alpha-cutout vegetation, and high-velocity particles:

let alpha_reactive = select(0.0, 1.0, is_alpha_tested); let water_reactive = select(0.0, 0.8, is_water_surface); let reactive_mask = max(alpha_reactive, water_reactive);

Before any GPU or CPU step executes, the solver enforces the strict 3D Courant–Friedrichs–Lewy (CFL) stability condition ($c \Delta t \sqrt{\Delta x^{-2} + \Delta y^{-2} + \Delta z^{-2}} \le 1$) and verifies discrete Poynting energy conservation:
