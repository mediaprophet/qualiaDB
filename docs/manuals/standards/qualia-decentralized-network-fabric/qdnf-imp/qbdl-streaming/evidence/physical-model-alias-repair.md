# Physical GGUF admission: canonical alias repair

The root run in core-integrated-gguf_bridge-gemma4.log passed 12 synthetic Gemma tests and failed four physical Gemma/Qwen smoke tests during admission. No physical forward execution passed in that run.

The existing supplier TensorIndex::try_build retains each original tensor and inserts a canonical alias when its name resolves to another role. The pinned tensor_roles::name_to_role used substring tests: attn_q_norm.weight resolved to the Q projection, attn_k_norm.weight to K, ffn_gate_inp.scale to the router, and ffn_down_exps.scale to expert-down. Thus physical directories gained duplicate projection/router hashes. The new Qualia Plan rejects duplicates; removing that check would silently choose the wrong tensor depending on ordering.

The bounded existing tools/diagnostics/gguf_inspect.py read all four physical metadata/tensor directories without reading their weight payloads. The report is physical-model-tensor-admission.json. The rejected Q weights are ordinary supported tensors: E4B [2560,2048] Q4_K, 26B [2816,4096] Q8_0, Qwen0.8B [1024,4096] Q4_K, Qwen4B [2560,8192] Q4_K. The corresponding Q/K norms are distinct one-dimensional weights. No new tensor layout, numerical kernel or architecture allowance is needed for this alias defect.

Repair scope in canonical Etalon and the pinned backport is src/tensor_roles.rs, src/inference/mod.rs, and new src/inference/tensor_alias_tests.rs. Roles require a .weight suffix and exact dot components, including the shared-expert qualifications. Fused QKV and gate-up aliases have the same weight/component boundary. Unknown norms/scales remain under their original hashes; legitimate projection and HF names retain their canonical roles. Current canonical Etalon's preexisting specialized exclusions remain intact and are not imported wholesale into the pinned package.

Qualia Gemma/Qwen plans, tensor shape/type checks, duplicate/unknown refusal, metadata bounds, and all-layer smoke assertions are untouched. Cargo and the pinned kernel identity are untouched. Canonical CHANGELOG.md has a concise entry; vendor PROVENANCE.md and BACKPORT-SHA256.json track the narrow additional backport.
