Mixer — Attention

Compose which body systems you attend to. Drag a fader to 0 to mute. Systems marked ·overlay (ECS / ENS / glymphatic) are distributed networks painted on host organs.

Parts

Every structure in this body, grouped by system. Untick a part to hide it.
Real HuBMAP/CCF reference-organ meshes, compiled to sealed .10d and shipped in one transparent .hmc bundle, rendered by the Qualia engine on WebGPU — the same renderer that runs natively on the desktop.
