Reference Body

The complete BodyParts3D body (CC BY-SA) is a large pack. Browsers can't fetch GitHub-release assets cross-origin, so:
↓ Download anatomy-bodyparts3d.qualia
then load the file:
The desktop app loads it directly — no download step.

Mixer — Attention

Compose which body systems you attend to — the faders are built from the systems this body actually contains. Drag a fader to 0 to mute a system. Systems marked ·overlay (ECS / ENS / glymphatic) are distributed networks with no single organ — evaluated and painted as an overlay on their host organs, so they're listed but not shown/hidden here.

Parts

Every individual structure this body contains, grouped by system. Untick a part to hide just that structure. Search to find one (the complete body has ~900).
Real HuBMAP/CCF reference-organ meshes, compiled to sealed .10d and shipped in one transparent .qualia bundle, rendered by the Qualia engine on WebGPU — the same renderer that runs natively on the desktop.
