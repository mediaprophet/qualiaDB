Device leases now reject known foreign queues and retain loss checks across shared ownership. Forward-arena readback refuses the wrong device and stale/lost owners, including loss after mapping completes. Cancellation and unmapping use RAII cleanup so buffers can be reused safely. Forge checks owner liveness before uploads and readbacks and refuses lost-device progress without advancing the weight cursor. Its large execution backend was split into focused files to separate lifecycle and execution responsibilities.

These changes preserve the 48-byte NQuin ABI, deterministic evaluation and caller-buffered hot paths. Cold construction remains bounded outside the Sentinel, with caller-selected budgets and fail-closed admission. Source consolidation preserves committed capability; it does not authorize stub replacement or removal of working inference.

Inference continuity and the next sprint

Existing production inference, real Webizen token acceptance and explicit independent developer/debug execution remain preserved. This non-inference closeout makes no claim that those inference modes were newly runtime-tested here. Qualia retains authenticated controller/policy checks and token/artifact acceptance; developer execution requires explicit admission and cannot silently replace missing authority. Persons, entities and agents may each have multiple identifiers; subject, identifier, controller and delegation remain distinct concepts.

P64 is the native model format. GGUF remains a compatibility/import route. FreeToken supplies reference architecture, not a dependency or replacement native format. Existing committed model functionality remains available. Further generic MoE and Qwen work, tokenizer/sampler integration, P64 inference product extensions and the independent optimization lab remain deferred EM-12–EM-15 work; they are neither declared complete nor removed from the programme.

Unpublished inference drafts were parked safely on inference-sprint-deferred-20261104 (retained tip 7d8b3362a), with both inference stashes preserved. They were not applied to the published non-inference closeout. The next sprint must reconcile those drafts with the immutable published pair, preserve working model paths, assemble the affected consumers and qualify actual family/state, mode, backend and publication behavior before cutover. Full-depth MoE/Qwen preservation, inference state equivalence, remaining product/lab integration and paired warmed-session performance remain future gates. Their deferral does not reopen the completed scoped non-inference closeout.

1. etalon-nquin: Single-Submit LSD Radix Sort & Coalesced Quin Sieve

crates/qualia-core-db/src/query/graph_accel/gpu.rs now delegates directly to etalon_nquin::GpuRadixSorter and etalon_nquin::GpuQuinSieve:

  • Zero Intermediate Readbacks: GpuRadixSorter::sort_u64_indices encodes all 8 four-bit LSD radix passes (histogram → prefix_scan → scatter) into a single GPU command submission with 8 uniform parameter buffers, eliminating per-pass CPU staging readbacks.
  • Warp-Transposed uvec4 Sieve: GpuQuinSieve::sieve_eq_indices casts &[NQuin] zero-copy to &[NQuinWire] (48-byte Pod) and performs coalesced 16-byte vector loads across workgroups.

2. etalon-render: Volumetric, EMF & Zero-Heap Flexbox HUD

Wired across qualia-core-db::render and re-exported by webizen-render for both native and wasm32:

  • EmfPipeline: Decoupled 4D electromagnetic field volumetric slicer (52-byte EmfFieldCell, 64-byte EmfSliceUniform) with VRAM ledger reservation guards.
  • EtalonVolumetricPipeline: 64-byte HolographicNodeGpu, 160-byte FrameCameraPod, and 48-byte ManifoldProjectionPod with bidirectional From conversions.
  • UiLayoutTree: Zero-heap stack-allocated Flexbox layout engine (MAX_HUD_LAYOUT_NODES = 128) driving QualiaHud::layout_items_flex(viewport_w, viewport_h).

3. etalon-tensor & etalon-wgsl-forge: Certified Inference Kernels

inference::etalon_tensor_bridge and wgsl_forge::etalon_bridge connect the pure-Rust tensor and shader compilers to SharedGpuContext:

  • ForwardArena & PagedKvBlockTable: Pre-allocated zero-heap transformer forward-pass scratch buffers and paged KV cache block tables.
  • FusedAttentionPipeline & TopkReductionPipeline: Online softmax rescaling with an explicit all-masked guard (prev_max == -INFINITY && block_max == -INFINITY) and 80-byte aligned LogitMaskHeader.
  • generate_and_certify_via_etalon: Compiles BuiltinKernel + Schedule to WGSL/SPIR-V/PTX/HLSL and runs Naga AST validation before dispatch.

4. etalon-agent & etalon-wasm: Hardware Lanes & Browser Steward

Deterministic resource governance from bare-metal PCIe/UMA to cross-origin isolated browser workers:

  • HalComputeLane & QualiaSlabAllocator: Exposed via SharedGpuContext::create_etalon_hal_lane() and create_etalon_slab_allocator() with SubmissionLease and SiliconCapability state machines.
  • WorkerSteward: Automatically negotiates SharedArrayBufferRing (when window.crossOriginIsolated === true) vs. TransferableMessageTransport fallback, tracking device loss and session recovery generations.

Pillar III · 10-Epic Game Engine & EcoPunk World Continuum

.10d Living Containers, PGA Motor Skinning, XPBD Fracture & 18 Vibe-0.1 Capabilities

qualia-core-db::scene_graph_10d and qualia-vibe-compiler provide the 10D spatial simulation and biological world-building capabilities below, under the zero-heap hot-path and bounded cold-construction contracts:

.10d Living Container Binary Format

Extending 64-byte aligned P64 containers with four new chunk types for procedural 10D assets:

  • PROC1 (0x0010): Deterministic 64-byte CSG + SDF procedural operator nodes.
  • EONT1 (0x0011): 48-byte NQuin ontological state machine rules embedded inside spatial assets.
  • MRP1 (0x0012): Quantized delta-compressed morph targets for continuous biological growth.
  • SKL1 (0x0013): 64-byte Projective Geometric Algebra ($\mathbf{Cl}(3,0,1)$) Motor bone hierarchies.

GPU PGA Dual-Quaternion Skinning & Motor IK

Artifact-free skeletal animation and procedural locomotion without heap allocation:

  • PGA Motor Blend Skinning: pga_skinning.rs blends 8-component motors $\mathbf{M} = \sum w_i \mathbf{M}_i$ with volume-preserving normalization and WGSL compute kernel generation.
  • 2D Delaunay Blend Spaces & ABSM: Barycentric triangle interpolation and 4-layer Animation Blueprint State Machines with inertialization transitions.
  • Two-Bone + FABRIK IK: Analytic hinge/pole knee solver + 8-iteration FABRIK chain solver with foot-placement terrain raycast alignment.

3D SDF + B-Rep XPBD Physics & Fracture

physics_3d_xpbd.rs replaces penalty springs with Extended Position-Based Dynamics:

  • Substepped Gauss-Seidel XPBD: Distance, bending, volume-conservation, and 6-DOF Cosserat rod constraints with compliance $\tilde{\alpha} = \alpha / \Delta t^2$.
  • Rank-2 Stress Tensor Cleavage: Tracks Cauchy stress $\sigma_{ij}$ per bond and cleaves structural links along principal stress planes when $\sigma_{\max} > \sigma_{\text{yield}}$.
  • Holosonic 3D Wavefield Audio: holosonic_audio.rs synthesizes material-coupled modal impact resonances, Woodhouse bow-string friction, and spherical-harmonic HRTF binaural audio.
