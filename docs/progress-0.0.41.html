1. etalon-nquin: Single-Submit LSD Radix Sort & Coalesced Quin Sieve

crates/qualia-core-db/src/query/graph_accel/gpu.rs delegates directly to etalon_nquin::GpuRadixSorter and etalon_nquin::GpuQuinSieve:

  • Zero Intermediate Readbacks: GpuRadixSorter::sort_u64_indices encodes all 8 four-bit LSD radix passes (histogram → prefix_scan → scatter) into a single GPU command submission with 8 uniform parameter buffers, eliminating per-pass CPU staging readbacks.
  • Warp-Transposed uvec4 Sieve: GpuQuinSieve::sieve_eq_indices casts &[NQuin] zero-copy to &[NQuinWire] (48-byte Pod) and performs coalesced 16-byte vector loads across workgroups.

2. etalon-render: Volumetric, EMF & Zero-Heap Flexbox HUD

Wired across qualia-core-db::render and re-exported by webizen-render for both native and wasm32:

  • EmfPipeline: Decoupled 4D electromagnetic field volumetric slicer (52-byte EmfFieldCell, 64-byte EmfSliceUniform) with VRAM ledger reservation guards.
  • EtalonVolumetricPipeline: 64-byte HolographicNodeGpu, 160-byte FrameCameraPod, and 48-byte ManifoldProjectionPod with bidirectional From conversions.
  • UiLayoutTree: Zero-heap stack-allocated Flexbox layout engine (MAX_HUD_LAYOUT_NODES = 128) driving QualiaHud::layout_items_flex(viewport_w, viewport_h).

3. etalon-tensor & etalon-wgsl-forge: Certified Compute & Inference Kernels

inference::etalon_tensor_bridge and wgsl_forge::etalon_bridge connect the pure-Rust tensor and shader compilers to SharedGpuContext:

  • ForwardArena & PagedKvBlockTable: Pre-allocated zero-heap transformer forward-pass scratch buffers and paged KV cache block tables with device-identity and liveness guards.
  • FusedAttentionPipeline & TopkReductionPipeline: Online softmax rescaling with an explicit all-masked guard (prev_max == -INFINITY && block_max == -INFINITY) and 80-byte aligned LogitMaskHeader.
  • generate_and_certify_via_etalon: Compiles BuiltinKernel + Schedule to WGSL/SPIR-V/PTX/HLSL and runs Naga AST validation before dispatch.

4. etalon-agent & etalon-wasm: Hardware Lanes & Browser Steward

Deterministic resource governance from bare-metal PCIe/UMA to cross-origin isolated browser workers:

  • HalComputeLane & QualiaSlabAllocator: Exposed via SharedGpuContext::create_etalon_hal_lane() and create_etalon_slab_allocator() with SubmissionLease foreign-queue rejection and SiliconCapability state machines.
  • WorkerSteward: Automatically negotiates SharedArrayBufferRing (when window.crossOriginIsolated === true) vs. TransferableMessageTransport fallback, tracking device loss and session recovery generations.
