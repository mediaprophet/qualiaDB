Why QualiaDB uses three DID methods instead of one overloaded identifier.

Most decentralized systems force a single DID method to act simultaneously as a database pointer, a human legal identity, and a network socket address. That overloading creates correlation honeypots and catastrophic revocation failure modes: rotating a compromised transport key Either breaks historical knowledge pointers or exposes the human across every peer relationship.

Orthogonal Separation by Design: QualiaDB partitions decentralized identifiers across three specialized methods — did:q42: (64-bit resource coordinates), did:webizen: (human-centric agent and ratified agreement identity), and did:qi: (pairwise rotating relationship and instrument identity). did:qi is an in-tree runtime specification (crates/qualia-core-db/src/did_qi/), not a W3C DID Spec Registries entry and not a W3C Technical Report.

Handle ≠ human. did:qi lives strictly on the Instrument and Handle planes — never the Entity (NaturalAgent) plane. Deactivating a did:qi relationship closes that pairwise channel without erasing the human or their local .q42 knowledge volume.

Layer 3 · Cryptographic Document Lifecycle

Bounded QCDE-1 Signed Documents & State Machine

Bounded QCDE-1 signed documents (Qualia Canonical Document Encoding v1, an RFC 8785 deterministic canonicalization profile) operate with fixed stack caps (MAX_CANONICAL, MAX_UNSIGNED, MAX_SIGNED, MAX_SERVICES, MAX_AKA) so parsing and signature verification never allocate on the heap in hot paths.

Step 1 · Genesis Creation method::create · encode_genesis

create & Genesis Digest

Computes the 32-byte SHA-256 genesis_digest over the initial controller verification key and creation vector (generation = 0), formats did:qi:z..., verifies the Ed25519 proof over encode_canonical, and commits the signed blob into the bounded QiStore / GitObjectStore.

Step 2 · Monotonic Rotation method::update · advance_generation

advance_generation & Replay Defense

Every key or service update must strictly increment the document generation counter (next.generation > current.generation) and carry a valid controller signature. Replayed or lower-generation documents fail closed with QiError::StaleGeneration ("stale_generation").

Step 3 · Terminal Tombstone method::deactivate · TombstonePermanent

deactivate & TombstonePermanent

Transitioning a relationship to deactivated = true writes an irreversible TombstonePermanent state (and supports optional UTXO TOMBSTONE_OPCODE attestation). Any subsequent update attempt returns QiError::Deactivated, and publish_qi_document immediately withdraws all mailbox services.

Step 4 · Plane Separation Guard verify_relationship_distinct_from_person

Strict Entity vs. Instrument Separation

verify_relationship_distinct_from_person enforces that a did:qi identifier is classified solely on the Instrument / Handle planes and never aliases a NaturalAgent Entity DID. Revoking a pairwise did:qi channel never invalidates the human’s identity.

Layer 4 · Transport Integration

CscpMailbox Binding & Disclosure Gates

Active did:qi documents publish CscpMailbox service endpoints into the private CSCP mailbox registry via publish_qi_document. Each service declares an explicit Disclosure policy and LocatorClass (Direct vs. RelayHint, up to MAX_HINTS):

  • Relay-only protection (check_relay_only): When a document specifies relay-only disclosure, any direct IP/socket locator is rejected before storage with QiError::DirectLocatorForbidden ("direct_locator_forbidden").
  • Direct-permitted selection: When direct locators are explicitly permitted by the controller’s policy, the path planner selects them as Direct without forcing traffic through a relay.
  • Deactivation scrubbing: Deactivated did:qi documents publish zero services and evict previously bound mailbox routes.

Verification & Standards Boundary

What did:qi Establishes in 0.0.41

  • In-process & zero-heap hot path: Canonical encoding, Ed25519 signature verification, in-memory GitObjectStore blob tagging, and caller-supplied Bitcoin-family UTXO commitment checks (verify_commitment) run deterministically in tree without external daemons.
  • Transition bearers stay labelled: Binding a CscpMailbox over TLS WSS, HTTP/2 Extended CONNECT capsules, WireGuard, or Nym mixnet framing uses those profiles as transition bearers — never claiming public Internet MASQUE or Native Independent link status.
  • Standards honesty: did:qi is an internal specification draft (0.1.0 on engine 0.0.41), not registered in the W3C DID Spec Registries.
